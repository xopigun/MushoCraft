# MushoCraft

A Minecraft mod inspired by the world of *Mushoku Tensei*: be born as a baby, grow up, and follow the path of magic, of the sword, or both.

> In development: playable, but balance and content are still moving.

## What is in it

- **Life**: born as a baby, you age and grow with game time; six natural talents are rolled at birth.
- **Magic**: a mana pool trained by use, 58 spells from Beginner to Emperor rank across the schools (attack, healing, detoxification, barrier, divine strike, summoning) plus mixed magic, chanted, shortened or silent casting, mastery per school, inherent magic.
- **The sword**: body training and combat aura, the Sword God, Water God and North God styles with their own movesets on Epic Fight weapons, and their named techniques.
- **Teachers and places**: sword masters and magic tutors who teach face to face, dojos and Milis churches generated in the world.
- **Monsters**: twelve species from the novel, each on its own Epic Fight skeleton or on Epic Fight's biped, with pack hunting and behaviour taken from the wiki — Assault Dog, Pax Coyote, Terminate Boar, Luster Grizzly, Goblin, Wraith, Armored Warrior, Mad Skull, Tarantula Deathlord, Snow Drake, Devouring Devil, Executioner — and the armour and shield made from Snow Drake scales.
- `/mushocraft max` makes a player as strong as possible in every discipline, for testing.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.263 or newer
- [Epic Fight](https://modrinth.com/mod/epic-fight) 21.17.3 or newer (required, installed separately — it is never bundled with MushoCraft)

## Building

```
./gradlew build
```

Gradle 8.8 needs a JDK 17 to run (`JAVA_HOME`); Java 21 for compiling is downloaded automatically.

Useful tasks:

| Task | What it does |
|---|---|
| `runClient` | Starts a development client with Epic Fight |
| `runClientTestWorld` | Opens the save `run/saves/mushotest` directly |
| `runGameTestServer` | Runs the automated in-game tests (`src/gametest`) and exits |
| `runClientGallery` | Films every showcase scene to `run/screenshots` (filter with `-Dmushocraft.gallery.only=<prefix>`) |

Monster models, animations and textures are generated by the Python scripts in `tools/bestiary` (`python build.py <creature>`), buildings by `tools/structures`.
